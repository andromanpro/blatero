# Recording, pilot terms and voice data

Before a work recording, notify participants or establish another applicable
basis under your policy. Blatero does not infer legal consent from speech.
The start checkbox records your statement, not a legal determination by software.

For example, explain before recording that an assistant will record/transcribe
and prepare a report, and ask participants to raise objections before it starts.
Whether that is sufficient depends on your actual context; use the full policy.

## Separate purposes

### The microphone button in a meeting app

Blatero captures the selected microphone independently. Your voice continues to
appear in its recording while Telemost or Kontur.Talk is muted. Use **Stop**
in Blatero to finish recording.

Automatic recording is enabled separately for each app in **Settings → Integrations**
with permission to start automatically. Windows microphone use by the app triggers
recording, including sound checks before joining. Inform participants before
recording begins according to the applicable rules.

- Work is for real meetings/decisions. Test is for experiments/training, not a
  blanket permission to record people without a basis.
- Recording permission is separate from enabling cloud processing.
- Saving a person's voiceprint requires a separate explicit decision/permission.
- A voice alias in a transcript is not automatic bank enrollment.

Meeting data can include two source audio tracks, optional video, transcript,
hints/draft/final report, assigned names and separately created voice profiles.
Check uncertain words, identities and conclusions before sharing results.
